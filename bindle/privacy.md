*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

This policy covers Bindle, a read-later app for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. In short: Bindle has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. What you save stays on your own devices.

### What Bindle collects

Nothing. The developer never receives your links, notes, highlights, reading history, or any information about you or your devices. Bindle has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

### Where your data lives

Your library is stored on each of your devices, in Bindle's app container. It syncs directly between devices you have paired, over your local network or Bluetooth. That traffic is end-to-end encrypted and mutually authenticated. No server or cloud service holds a copy, and iCloud is not used.

### What Bindle connects to

- **The pages you save.** Bindle fetches each page, its images, and its title and preview so you can read it offline. These requests go directly to the site that hosts the page and send no cookies and no referrer. The site sees them as it would any visit from your device.
- **Natural voice files.** If you turn on the natural voice, Bindle downloads the voice model and language data once from Hugging Face and GitHub, where they are published. Nothing about you or your library is sent with those downloads.
- **Your other devices.** Sync happens only between devices you have paired.

### Device permissions

Each of these is used only on the device and only for the purpose shown. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

- **Local network and Bluetooth:** to find your other devices and sync with them.
- **Camera:** on iPhone and iPad, to scan a pairing code. On any device, only if you turn on the reader's look-away pause, to notice when you look away. On iPhone and iPad this uses face tracking, which is processed on the device and never stored.
- **Microphone:** to hear the pairing sound from your other device while pairing.
- **Motion:** only if you turn on tilt scrolling in the reader.

### Children

Bindle collects no data from anyone, including children.
